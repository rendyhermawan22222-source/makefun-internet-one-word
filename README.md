One word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins. Free to enter, no files, judged two at a time by a model.

Our word is **brainrot**: the whole internet in eight letters. Every feed, comment section, rabbit hole and 3 a.m. scroll is one machine doing the same thing to your head — and the word itself is funny before you even think about why. It is not one app, one site or one mood; it is the entire internet's effect, and it lands in a single beat.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
